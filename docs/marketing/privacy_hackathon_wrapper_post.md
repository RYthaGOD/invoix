# Introducing Invoix Shield: The Confidential Layer for $INVOIX

**We are building a second application specifically for the [Privacy Hackathon Name/Solana Privacy Hackathon].**

Privacy isn't just a feature; it's a fundamental requirement for business operations. While Invoix brings invoice settlement on-chain, businesses need the option to transact without broadcasting their entire financial history to the world.

## Enter Invoix Shield (Draft Name)

We are developing a standalone **Confidential SPL (c-SPL) Token Wrapper** designed to bring true privacy to the Invoix ecosystem while driving massive utility to the $INVOIX token.

### 🔒 The Tech: Solana Token-2022 Confidential Extensions
Leveraging the cutting-edge **Token-2022 Confidential Transfer** extensions, this wrapper allows users to:
1.  **Wrap** their public $INVOIX and stablecoins into confidential counterparts (e.g., $cINVOIX).
2.  **Transact** with fully encrypted balances and transfer amounts.
3.  **Audit** their own transaction history without exposing it to the public ledger.

This isn't a mixer. It's compliant, audit-ready privacy using Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) native to Solana.

### 🔥 The Utility: Burning $INVOIX on Every Interaction
We are hyper-focused on value accrual for the $INVOIX token. This new application is designed as a **deflationary engine**:

*   **Wrap Fee**: A small fee in $INVOIX is required to wrap assets. **100% of this fee is BURNED.**
*   **Privacy Transfer Fee**: Every confidential transfer burns a fraction of $INVOIX.
*   **Unwrap Fee**: Exiting privacy mode burns $INVOIX.

**Invoix Shield** doesn't just protect your data; it actively reduces the supply of $INVOIX, aligning privacy incentives with token holder value.

### 🏗️ Why Build This?
Invoix is for serious business. Businesses demand privacy. By building this as a modular wrapper:
*   We keep the core Invoix protocol lightweight and transparent.
*   We offer an "opt-in" privacy layer for users who need it.
*   We create a second, powerful utility sink for the $INVOIX token.
